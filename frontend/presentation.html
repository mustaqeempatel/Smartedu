Team Infinity X • Core Innovation

The 4-Tier Confidence Mismatch Matrix

Separating mere guessing from deep-seated conceptual misconceptions

Mandatory Post-Answer Confidence

After answering every question, students must rate their confidence: Not Sure, Somewhat Sure, Confident, or Very Confident.

A student who answers incorrectly while being Very Confident has built a false mental model. They do not know they are wrong. This is the highest-priority academic support signal.
CORRECT + HIGH CONF
Mastery 🌟

Understands and applies concepts with certainty.

WRONG + HIGH CONF
Misconception ⚠️

Strongest support signal! False belief in incorrect rule.

CORRECT + LOW CONF
Hesitation ❓

Got it right but doubts ability. Needs reassurance.

WRONG + LOW CONF
Knowledge Gap 📉

Knows they don't know. Needs first-time instruction.

Team Infinity X • Algorithmic Engine

Learning Support Signal Engine

Multi-factor mathematical formulation calibrated for high precision

FACTOR 1 (0-35 PTS)
Error Frequency

Concentration of repeated mistakes on a topic. If topic error rate ≥ 60%, triggers distress factor.

FACTOR 2 (0-20 PTS)
Repeated Attempts

Number of tries taken on questions within the same concept without score stabilization.

FACTOR 3 (0-25 PTS)
Latency Contrast

Compares average response time on topic against student baseline on other topics (ratio > 1.2x).

FACTOR 4 (0-25 PTS)
Confidence Mismatch

Overconfidence on incorrect answers weighted heavily (10 pts per mismatch) to target misconceptions.

Support Score = min(100, Error Pts + Attempt Pts + Latency Pts + Mismatch Pts) Threshold: Score ≥ 45 ➜ Generates "REVIEW" Signal
